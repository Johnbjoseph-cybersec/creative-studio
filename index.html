The publishing tool

The studio uses a private, internal tool to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account, and to read the performance statistics of those accounts. The tool is used only by the studio, for accounts the studio owns. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
